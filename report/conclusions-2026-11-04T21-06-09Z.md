*Conclusions & next steps*
- Pilot `jev-predicate` as the default: it matches chat’s 422 correct classifications at ~98% lower reported cost. Chat accounts for ~79% of the $11.09 total spend; reserve it for targeted fallback tests.
- Use `jev-choice` only if budget outweighs accuracy: ~32% cheaper than `jev-predicate`, but only 30 ms faster and 5 percentage points less accurate. `openai-choice` is fastest, but saves just another 20 ms.
- Deprioritize score variants: both cost more and are slower than their corresponding choice variants, while delivering lower accuracy.
- `openai-predicate` costs ~6.5× more than `jev-predicate`, is ~16% slower, and reports more errors. Investigate its 12.39M prompt tokens before further optimization.
- Validate on identical ad sets with missing predictions counted as failures: denominators differ, so 97.2% vs 96.8% is not a clean accuracy win. Measure end-to-end p95 latency and retry-inclusive cost; confirm reported prices include comparable serving costs.
